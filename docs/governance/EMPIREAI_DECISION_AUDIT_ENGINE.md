## Constitutional Purpose

Every executive decision shall be fully auditable. The Decision Audit Engine provides complete traceability from original executive context through recommendation, approval, execution and final outcome. Evidence, reasoning, approvals and impacts are permanently preserved. Nothing becomes an unexplained executive action.
