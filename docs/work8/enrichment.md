# Source enrichment and candidate-bound assessment linkage

Candidate implementation, not a production release or Work 8 closure. The existing intelligence tick invokes a durable per-parent progression through the existing CJ detail, stock and freight ReadAcquirer paths. No additional timer, credential lookup, live provider call or inference execution was used in these local examinations. The unchanged provider quota remains shared with baseline reads and fresh discovery; 100 discoveries do not imply 100 enrichments or qualifications.

Each tick admits at most one job, with one pending enrichment progression, ten parent generations/day and twenty enrichment jobs/day. Parent state, queued job and digest-linked receipt/head commit atomically. Readers verify retained job request bindings and receipt-derived state. Failed, partially completed or interrupted jobs defer without automatic replay. Source references bind complete evidence envelopes, exact parent/variant/destination operands and freshness. Initial source envelopes must match the discovery receipt that introduced the parent.

A sole verified variant can progress; ambiguous variants and absent explicit destinations defer. The default existing CJ schedule does not supply a destination, so its source candidates cannot silently assume one. Unknown warehouse, shipping or commercial fields are not populated with guessed values. Completed logistics produces an evidence-completeness record using the existing intelligence qualifier; this is distinct from the canonical commerce qualifier and conveys no commercial approval. Seven reviewed commercial dimensions, complete economics, cash/liabilities/FX, media rights and any genuinely completed assessment remain explicit evidence requirements.

Refresh checks examine at most 32 deferred parents per tick, with at most 20 retained detail sources each, using a durable rotating cursor. Later fresh exact-parent detail evidence may open a new generation, up to three generations per parent; earlier receipts are retained unchanged. The limit is explicit and requires reviewed continuation beyond it. Unprocessed identities use an indexed bounded anti-join against persisted verified per-parent progress, rather than reading only the first 1,000 identities. An isolated test retains 1,001 deferred parents and successfully admits the next identity at zero qualified products. The 1,000 active-commerce limit is unchanged. Independent defensive verification limits remain 10,000 enrichment receipts/states and the discovery ledger's own history bound; capacity exhaustion fails closed and requires reviewed archival work, not a success declaration.

An optional `candidateId` on the existing authenticated investigation grant pins product identity, subject and exact evidence-envelope snapshot. The context cannot silently omit pinned evidence when its payload bound is exceeded. No new inference authority is granted. Completion preserves that binding; historical unbound investigations stay unlinked. Read-only linkage checks the current pinned evidence, completed investigation, recorded rounds and delivered evidence, durable completed request/response hash, and authenticated-owner closure. It does not establish the semantic correctness of the assessment, profitability, approval or an owner recommendation.

The existing authenticated Portfolio endpoint supplies the owner readback. Its UI separates discovery, enrichment, deferral and completed-assessment linkage, returns no full chat text, and states sample limits: latest 20 enrichment records, latest 100 investigations and at most five request proofs. Unknown/unavailable evidence does not become a zero or success.

## Remaining restrictions

No per-parent completed Pillow assessment has been generated by this code change. Existing grant, budget and inference authorization remain mandatory. Authentic rights, independent reviewed commercial dimensions, complete exact-variant shipping/economic facts and verified financial inputs must exist before commercial qualification can succeed. The isolated proof-link fixture is not an owner recommendation. These restrictions remain visible and must not be relabelled as successful qualification.

The automatic routine persists a canonical-input deferral record (missing schema fields, qualified false, canonicalEngineInvoked false). It does not manufacture a complete commercial candidate. `evaluateCompleteEnrichment` is a separate nonpersisted read-only helper, normalizing cash/liabilities to unknown, FX false and reviewed evidence false before reusing the canonical engine, matching the existing real-research route. It is not an operational successful-qualification claim.

Final review hardening adds a durable context-delivery receipt (request ID, binding hash, exact evidence IDs, snapshot digest and time). Candidate completion without that receipt refuses. Read-only linkage rejects empty answers, mismatched closure/grant owners and invalid or future chronology. Candidate grant existence and duplicate-instruction checks share one SQLite transaction with source-binding validation and insertion; a real two-process connection race admits exactly one grant. These prove technical delivery and linkage only, not semantic model reading.
