## Primary sources

- https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/report-type-values-analytics : SQP/Search Catalog require Brand Analytics and Brand Registry; Search Terms has further eligibility. Report-list access proves neither dataset eligibility nor metrics.
- https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/reference/searchcatalogitems : product discovery.
- https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/reference/getmyfeesestimateforasin : product, marketplace and proposed-price fee estimates.
- https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/reference/getlistingsrestrictions : seller-specific restrictions.
- https://developers.cjdropshipping.com/en/api/api2/standard/points.html : catalog50points; detail/stock/freight10each; UTC reset and account inactivity suspension.
- https://developers.cjdropshipping.com/en/api/api2/api/logistic.html : variant/origin/destination/quantity freight estimates.
- https://keepa.com/api-docs/plans-tokens.html : monthly API token subscription; product1token; optional offers cost more; illustrated example prices are not current quotes.
- https://developers.google.com/search/apis/trends : restricted alpha, interest proxy not absolute demand.
- https://www.cpsc.gov/Recalls/CPSC-Recalls-Application-Program-Interface-API-Information : legitimate public recall data.

## Resource decision

No third-party bundle purchase. Keepa is a focused historical-data candidate with adapter prepared. Public pricing inspected directly at https://keepa.com/#!api/3: Starter API, 20 tokens/minute, EUR49/month prepaid. Account tax and FX remain unknown. At the bounded initial ceiling of100 tokens/day, even this minimum plan is substantially oversized; defer purchase until a specific historical investigation warrants it. Accumulated first-party snapshots can support core reads; historical-dependent conclusions stay incomplete. No new material recurring charge authorized or incurred. Rejections remain durable rather than prompting repeatedly.
