# Cross-Empire Governance Engine

Doctrine: PILLOW-CEG-001. Mission: X5-11.

The Cross-Empire Governance Engine establishes unified constitutional governance across the Empire. It manages enterprise-wide governance policies and constitutional rules, validates compliance, monitors consistency, detects violations and policy conflicts, evaluates governance risks, and generates governance recommendations.

It produces machine-readable governance records with status, health, and failure reporting. It never exposes credentials or authentication tokens, never bypasses constitutional governance, and never approves non-compliant operations automatically. Governance traceability, auditability, and enterprise integrity are preserved.
