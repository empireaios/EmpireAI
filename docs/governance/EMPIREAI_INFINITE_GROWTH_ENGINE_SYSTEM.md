# Infinite Growth Engine

Doctrine: PILLOW-IGE-001. Mission: X5-19.

The Infinite Growth Engine establishes sustainable long-term enterprise growth. It monitors long-term enterprise growth; evaluates scalability plus governance and operational sustainability; detects long-term growth constraints and governance/operational risks; ranks sustainable growth opportunities; and generates long-term growth recommendations.

It produces machine-readable growth records with status, health, and failure reporting. It never exposes credentials or authentication tokens, never sacrifices constitutional governance for growth, and never reduces operational quality to increase growth. Growth traceability, auditability, and enterprise integrity are preserved.
