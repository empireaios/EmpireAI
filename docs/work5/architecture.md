# Work5 financial evidence architecture

The canonical historical financial ledger and inference calls are read-only sources. A native SQLite evidence/reconciliation journal under the original volume augments those sources: it does not repost their amounts or execute payments. Stable economic-event identity and replacement links mean accrual, invoice and settlement are one event. Reviews and reversals are append-only. Unknown legacy authenticity stays excluded. Existing provider reservation accounting remains authoritative for admission; an owner-configured monthly native-USD limit may further restrict it, never enlarge existing authority.

All-in coverage uses an extensible provider catalogue. Evidence packages carry original units, source hashes and dates, FX, taxes, credits, attribution and source IDs. Manual source review is explicitly owner attestation, not independent bank verification. Missing billing/FX/bank coverage cannot become zero or verified cash. Reviewed cash-account coverage is separate from individual statements. Historical fixtures remain inspectable.

Deterministic economics and forecasts never call an LLM or qualify products. Saved forecasts link to reviewed outcome evidence, lessons and revised assumptions in current Pillow context. This provides an outcome-linked capability, not proof of compounded commercial performance. Advisor financial_centre reads the same projection. Finance controls are authenticated founder/workspace scoped and cannot execute commerce.

Safety gates: schema validation, finite bounded micro-units, chronological evidence, content hashes, explicit owner review, duplicate invoice/source denial, same-origin frontend POST, immutable receipts, SQLite FULL sync and reopen tests. Pricing expiry remains fail-closed; existing October8 reviewed tariffs are reused, with7-day alerts rather than an unreviewed renewal. Future off-volume restore, full console redesign and longitudinal performance certification retain their Work6/7/8 ownership.
