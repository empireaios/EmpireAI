## Purpose

The Winning Product Detector is the product opportunity engine for EmpireAI. It continuously identifies products that demonstrate exceptional scaling potential — monitoring performance, sales velocity, revenue and profit growth, demand, conversion, trends, and inventory movement — and detects breakout and declining products early.
