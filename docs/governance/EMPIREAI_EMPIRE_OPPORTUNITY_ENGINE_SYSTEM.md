# EmpireAI Empire Opportunity Engine System

**Doctrine:** PILLOW-EOP-001  
**Mission:** X5-06  
**Module:** `empire-opportunity-engine`

The Empire Opportunity Engine records only structural opportunity signals. It does not acquire external intelligence, make factual market claims, access credentials, or execute enterprise actions.

Recommendations are permitted only when their originating opportunity record is explicitly validated. The safety invariant `neverRecommendOpportunitiesUsingUnvalidatedIntelligence` is forced to `true`, with traceability and auditability retained for every record.

The engine connects to the Empire Intelligence Framework as a planned X5-06 intelligence module while retaining module isolation.
