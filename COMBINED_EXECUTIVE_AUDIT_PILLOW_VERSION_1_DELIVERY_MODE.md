## 3. Execution rule

Every remaining Pillow mission **shall** declare:

- **Which Version 1 certification blocker it removes** (B5–B8); **or**
- **Which approved Product Integration Phase (1–3) it completes**

If neither condition is satisfied → **defer** until after Version 1 executive certification.

**Dual gate:** Pillow missions must also satisfy Empire **Certification Mode** (ADR-048).
