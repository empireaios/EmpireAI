## Purpose

The Company Factory Certification suite validates the complete Company Factory programme — framework, opportunity discovery, market validation, business model generation, brand creation, domain planning, store generation, product portfolio, pricing, launch readiness, launch orchestration, growth initialization, launch monitoring, and first revenue optimization — and produces machine-readable certification reports.

## Scope

Strictly limited to Company Factory certification (X1-15). Does not create new businesses outside certification evidence, modify production systems outside safe test mode, or extend beyond Company Factory programme validation.

## Safety

- Never expose credentials or authentication tokens  
- Never modify production systems during certification unless explicitly configured for safe test mode  
- Structural signals only — certification evidence from module probes and records  
- Preserve operational traceability, auditability, and certification integrity  
- Sensitive values redacted from logs
