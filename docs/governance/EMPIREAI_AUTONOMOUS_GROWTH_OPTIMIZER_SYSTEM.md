## Purpose

The Autonomous Growth Optimizer module provides continuous autonomous growth optimization for EmpireAI. It monitors enterprise, revenue, profit, customer, and operational growth; identifies opportunities and constraints; optimizes sustainable growth strategies; ranks priorities; and recommends autonomous growth using structural signals only — never optimizing growth beyond validated operational limits.
