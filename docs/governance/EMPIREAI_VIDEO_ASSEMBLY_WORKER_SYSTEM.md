# EmpireAI Video Assembly Worker

PILLOW-VAW-001 / Q4-11 provides the Video Assembly Worker.

The Video Assembly Worker assembles every approved media asset into a finished production-ready video. It synchronizes scripts, voiceovers, visuals, graphics, music, captions and transitions into a coherent final video. It produces assembled videos as structural signals (timeline, render settings, output format refs — not binary video blobs). It does **not** write scripts, generate voiceovers or thumbnails, or publish media.

## Authority

The Video Assembly Worker:

- operates as an isolated AI Worker within the Media Factory
- receives approved scripts, voice, visual, creative, and music assets
- synchronizes narration and visuals on a scene timeline
- applies scene transitions and motion effects
- produces multiple output resolutions and aspects
- validates rendering quality and produces machine-readable Video Assembly Reports
- submits reports through the Executive Reporting Runtime
- operates autonomously under Pillow governance

## Boundaries

The Video Assembly Worker never:

- writes scripts
- generates voiceovers
- generates thumbnails
- publishes media
- overrides Pillow
- overrides Grand King
- implements Q4-12 or later

## Mandatory rules

- Preserve complete asset traceability
- Preserve synchronization between media assets
- Validate rendering quality
- Preserve audit history
- Submit reports through the Executive Reporting Runtime
- Never publish videos directly
