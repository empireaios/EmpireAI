# EmpireAI International Executive Cockpit

**System ID:** PILLOW-IEC-001  
**Mission:** X4-18

The International Executive Cockpit provides machine-readable, structural executive snapshots of worldwide KPI, regional, country, opportunity, risk, and recommendation signals. It is not a source of production authority or restricted enterprise intelligence.

## Mandatory safety controls
- Credentials, authentication tokens, and restricted enterprise information are never exposed.
- All records preserve executive traceability and auditability.
- Output is structural-signal-only; sensitive values are masked and never logged.
- Unvalidated signals cannot authorize enterprise action.
