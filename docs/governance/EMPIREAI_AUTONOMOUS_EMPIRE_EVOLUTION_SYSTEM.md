# Autonomous Empire Evolution

Doctrine: PILLOW-AEE-001. Mission: X5-17.

The Autonomous Empire Evolution engine establishes continuous enterprise evolution. It evaluates enterprise structures, workflows, and business models; detects structural, workflow, and business-model evolution opportunities; simulates proposed evolutions; ranks evolution priorities; generates evolution recommendations; and tracks evolution outcomes.

It produces machine-readable evolution records with status, health, and failure reporting. It never exposes credentials or authentication tokens, never modifies governance-approved enterprise architecture automatically, and never bypasses constitutional governance. Evolution traceability, auditability, and enterprise integrity are preserved.
