> **Canonical owner:** Pillow Architecture  
> **Authority:** BL-C · `EMPIREAI_BL_C_CONTINUOUS_IMPROVEMENT_CONSTITUTION.md`  
> **Repository artifact:** `docs/governance/PILLOW_ENHANCEMENT_REGISTER.md`  
> **Status:** ACTIVE (opened 2026-06-29)  
> **Primary reference:** `PILLOW_ARCHITECTURE_CONTRACT.md` Part 7 (finalized) · **`PILLOW_ROADMAP.md`** · **`EMPIREAI_PILLOW_EXECUTIVE_INTELLIGENCE_CONSTITUTION.md` (Layer 2 Core Principle)**

This document permanently records approved future enhancements for **completed Pillow modules**.

It is a canonical repository artifact. It does **not** modify the frozen Pillow architecture contract.

Registration preserves future product improvements so they are never lost during future conversations.

Registration does **not** authorize engineering execution. Grand King approval is required for promotion to missions.

**Roadmap layer:** Enhancements in this register primarily belong to **Pillow Executive Intelligence (Layer 2)** unless they are runtime transport/hosting concerns (Layer 1 — complete). All Layer 2 enhancements shall serve the Core Principle and **Supreme Directive alignment** in `EMPIREAI_PILLOW_EXECUTIVE_INTELLIGENCE_CONSTITUTION.md` §1 · §1.1. **Executive Cognitive Pipelines (ADR-046)** organise PEI missions into pipelines A–E for Master Plan prioritisation — see `docs/governance/EXECUTIVE_COGNITIVE_PIPELINES.md`. **Executive Reflection (PEI-026)** is the first downstream Layer 2 capability after Bootstrap — candidates only, GK approval required. **Continuous Artifact Generation Workflow** (`EMPIREAI_CONTINUOUS_ARTIFACT_GENERATION_WORKFLOW.md`) is the default development workflow — lasting decisions shall produce repository artifacts before implementation. Evidence-Based Learning missions (PEI-021) shall treat all learning inputs as **Evidence Sources** and respect §3.2 — no Evidence Source may directly modify Executive Knowledge. See `PILLOW_ROADMAP.md` § Layer 2.
