## 1. Purpose

The Core Constitution (CTD) is the **single permanent commercial constitution** of EmpireAI.

P2-02 **ratifies** CTD-001→CTD-040 into their permanent constitutional role. This mission **does not rewrite** immutable articles. It **validates**, **consolidates interpretation**, and **binds** all subordinate law to one apex.

**The principle:** One constitutional authority · one constitutional interpretation · one constitutional hierarchy · every future constitutional document derives authority from the CTD · no future document may contradict the CTD.
