## What the S candidate does and does not establish

The S controller protects one authoritative journal, admits its own direct children, waits for durable private-IPC acknowledgement before application import, records actual process identity and retains current/prior membership. Its hosted examination targets four synthetic direct roles and generation refusal. Its per-journal singleton does not prove that no other journal/controller or external Redis producer has write access. A passing S examination therefore supplies a primitive and isolated evidence, not complete production membership.

The retained actual process graph is more complex: locked-runtime runs supervised observers and a launcher-loss guard; the guard starts the primary; the primary starts/replaces the brain worker. The launcher itself writes an inference startup readback. locked-supervision records containment audit data. Both observers have capture and restart semantics. These controller-side mutations must not disappear from the census merely because they are not one of the four synthetic roles.

## Smallest credible source correction

Use one privileged lifecycle controller with no ordinary application write capability. Move every actual application mutation behind runtime admission. Keep established supervision deadlines, progress monitoring, capture protocols and admission fences. Avoid replacing the entire launcher or duplicating a second recovery framework.
