# EmpireAI Platform Certification System

## Doctrine
PILLOW-PFC-001 governs Q6-15 Platform Certification. This system evaluates Q6-01 through Q6-14 from observed repository, runtime, safety, governance, and controlled-fixture evidence.

## Non-negotiable boundaries
- Certification is never fabricated or auto-marked complete.
- It never activates real production, conducts real customer billing, or overrides Pillow or Grand King authority.
- Q7-01 and later are outside this mission.
- A `Certified` result requires every mandatory mission gate, safety check, and controlled end-to-end outcome to be observed as successful.

## Evidence
Evidence is read-only where practical. Missing worker handles, missing prior FINAL PASS evidence, failed probes, and unobserved scenario outcomes are reported honestly and reduce certification status.
