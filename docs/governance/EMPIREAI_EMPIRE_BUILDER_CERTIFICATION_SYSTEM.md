# EmpireAI Empire Builder Certification

PILLOW-EBC-001 / Q2-10 provides the Empire Builder Certification.

This is the final certification mission for Q2. Its purpose is to verify that the complete Empire Builder Factory operates correctly as one integrated business-planning organization under Pillow.

The Empire Builder Certification is the executive acceptance gate for Q2. Only after Q2 passes certification should implementation proceed to Q3.

> Note: Doctrine ID is **PILLOW-EBC-001**. There is one authoritative Empire Builder Certification service. This service is the final acceptance gate for Q2 before Q3 begins.

## Boundaries

The Empire Builder Certification:

- **does** validate the complete Empire Builder Factory, verify integration between all Q2 components, produce the final Empire Builder Certification Report, and confirm readiness for Q3
- does **not** execute business implementation
- does **not** modify factory components
- does **not** repair failures automatically
- does **not** begin Q3 implementation
- does **not** override Pillow
- does **not** override Grand King

## Components certified

Q2-01 Empire Builder Factory Core through Q2-09 Business Approval Pack Worker.

## Certification report

Each report includes: Certification ID, Timestamp, Empire Builder Factory Version, Original Grand King Command, Components Tested, Components Passed, Components Failed, Integration Status, Planning Completeness, Governance Compliance, Outstanding Risks, Recommendations, Final Certification Result, and Metadata version (`EBC-001-v1`).

## Certification levels

Default: certified, certified_with_warnings, provisionally_certified, failed_certification.

Additional certification levels can be registered through configuration without redesign.

## Mandatory validation

Grand King business command accepted, business intent generated, business model generated, market research completed, opportunity evaluated, business blueprint completed, launch plan completed, business risks assessed, Business Approval Pack generated, full traceability preserved, executive reporting completed, and the entire Empire Builder Factory remains governed by Pillow.

## Safety

Credentials and authentication tokens are never exposed. Certification operations preserve auditability and traceability. Sensitive values are masked in logs. Certification reports never claim that the service executed business implementation, modified factory components, repaired failures, began Q3, overrode Pillow, or overrode Grand King.
