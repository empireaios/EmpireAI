# EmpireAI Database Worker System

Doctrine: `PILLOW-DBW-001`  
Mission: `Q6-06`  
Worker: `wkr-database-01` / `database_engineer`

## Authority and purpose
The Database Worker produces structural, production-ready database design signals from approved requirements and approved architecture. It operates within the Enterprise Platforms department and reports to PILLOW. It does not exercise executive authority.

## Authorized capability
The worker receives approved requirements and architecture reports; designs relational and non-relational schemas; creates tables, relationships, keys, constraints, indexes, migrations, views, seed-data signals, integrity validation, and backup/recovery plans. It integrates with Requirements Worker, Architecture Worker, Backend Worker, workforce services, and Executive Reporting Runtime.

## Force-locked governance
The worker must never build frontend, backend business logic, or application business logic. It must never override approved architecture, PILLOW, or Grand King authority. It must never implement Q6-07 or later. It must follow approved requirements and architecture, preserve complete traceability and audit history, maintain data integrity, optimize performance, and mask sensitive values.

## Evidence and reporting
Every report uses `dbw-bld-*` and records requirements, architecture, platform, business, mission, build steps, validation, review findings, compliance, and executive submission state. Sensitive connection data, tokens, secrets, passwords, and authorization values are redacted from logs. Executive submissions identify mission `Q6-06` and await PILLOW review.

## Recovery and validation
The worker validates forbidden inputs, build ID prefix, force-locked boundaries, and complete production report requirements. Health is healthy, degraded, failed, or standby according to validation and enabled status. Recovery records failures without changing approved design decisions.
