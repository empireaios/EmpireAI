# EmpireAI Global Risk Intelligence System

**System:** PILLOW-GRI-001  
**Mission:** X4-15

Global Risk Intelligence produces structural risk signals for geopolitical, economic,
regulatory, operational, logistics, financial, regional-business, and emerging
international risk categories. It ranks records and produces mitigation
recommendations only from validated intelligence.

## Governance invariants

- Never expose credentials or authentication tokens.
- Never suppress critical international risks.
- Preserve risk traceability, auditability, and enterprise integrity.
- Use structural signals only; sensitive values are masked and never logged.
- Never make decisions using unvalidated risk intelligence.

This subsystem does not call live risk APIs or execute mitigation decisions.
