## Minimum Requirements (Summary)

1. Every completed mission with accepted **PROOF** produces **lessons learned** (3+ bullets).  
2. Pillow evaluates accumulation per **classification** (PV · EP · BP · AP · OP · HE · RI · DI).  
3. **Permanent Vision** changes require **Grand King approval**.  
4. No silent Vision edits — all PV via register + revision history.
