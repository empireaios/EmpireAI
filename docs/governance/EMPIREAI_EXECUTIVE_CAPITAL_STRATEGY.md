## Constitutional Purpose

Enterprise valuation measures what the Empire is worth. Executive Capital Strategy governs how capital will be preserved, allocated and deployed over the long term. EmpireAI shall continuously define, monitor and explain long-term capital strategy — allocation priorities, investment horizons, preservation vs growth balance and strategic deployment — anchored to enterprise valuation.
