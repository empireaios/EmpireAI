# Autonomous Investment Engine

Doctrine: PILLOW-AIE-001. Mission: X5-12.

The Autonomous Investment Engine establishes enterprise-wide investment intelligence. It discovers and evaluates investment opportunities, estimates expected returns, assesses risks, prioritizes opportunities, recommends investment strategies, executes only governance-approved strategies, monitors investment performance, and detects underperforming investments.

It produces machine-readable investment records with status, health, and failure reporting. It never exposes credentials or authentication tokens, and never executes investments without governance approval. Investment traceability, auditability, and enterprise integrity are preserved.
