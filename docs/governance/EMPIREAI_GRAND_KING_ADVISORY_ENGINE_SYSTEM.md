# Grand King Advisory Engine

Doctrine: PILLOW-GKA-001. Mission: X5-15.

The Grand King Advisory Engine establishes continuous executive strategic advisory. It analyzes enterprise performance, identifies strategic opportunities and risks, prioritizes executive decisions, recommends capital allocation, growth, optimization, and governance actions, and tracks advisory outcomes.

It produces machine-readable advisory records with status, health, and failure reporting. It never exposes credentials or authentication tokens, and never executes executive decisions automatically without approved governance. Advisory traceability, auditability, and enterprise integrity are preserved.
