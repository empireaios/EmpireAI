# EmpireAI Subtitle Worker

PILLOW-STW-001 / Q4-12 provides the Subtitle Worker.

The Subtitle Worker generates accurate captions, subtitles, transcripts and synchronized timing files for every approved media production. It maximizes accessibility, searchability and multilingual support. It produces subtitle assets as structural signals (cue timelines, export path descriptors — not binary media blobs). It does **not** rewrite scripts, assemble videos, or publish content.

## Authority

The Subtitle Worker:

- operates as an isolated AI Worker within the Media Factory
- receives approved scripts and voice assets
- generates complete transcripts and synchronized captions
- generates subtitle timing and multi-language subtitle sets
- validates timing accuracy and detects synchronization issues
- produces exportable SRT, VTT, TXT transcript, and caption timeline refs
- produces machine-readable Subtitle Reports
- submits reports through the Executive Reporting Runtime
- operates autonomously under Pillow governance

## Boundaries

The Subtitle Worker never:

- rewrites scripts
- modifies approved scripts
- assembles videos
- publishes content
- overrides Pillow
- overrides Grand King
- implements Q4-13 or later

## Mandatory rules

- Preserve script traceability
- Preserve subtitle synchronization
- Preserve transcript history
- Validate subtitle quality
- Preserve audit history
- Submit reports through the Executive Reporting Runtime
- Never modify approved scripts
