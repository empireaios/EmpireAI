## Constitutional Purpose

Executive decisions shall not rely solely on human judgment or historical experience. They shall also be governed by executive policy. Executive policies define the rules that guide executive behaviour across the Empire. Every executive decision is automatically validated against these policies before execution. Policies evolve safely while preserving constitutional integrity.
