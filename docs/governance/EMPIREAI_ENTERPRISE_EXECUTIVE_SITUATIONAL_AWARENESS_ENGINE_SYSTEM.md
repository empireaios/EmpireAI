# EmpireAI Enterprise Executive Situational Awareness Engine

PILLOW-EESAE-001 / EESAE-01 provides the Enterprise Executive Situational Awareness Engine (EESAE) inside Pillow.

EESAE is a foundational, permanent, post-Q constitutional capability. It changes Pillow from a passive assistant that waits for instructions into executive intelligence that continuously maintains awareness of EmpireAI operational state. The Grand King must not be required to discover problems first — Pillow discovers them first.

## Constitutional Duty

Continuous executive situational awareness is a permanent Digital Soul responsibility (`constitutionalDutyActive: true`). EESAE observes, analyses, recommends, escalates, and reports. It never fabricates metrics, never silently ignores critical deterioration when evidence exists, never auto-modifies production, and never bypasses Pillow or Grand King governance.

## Workflow

1. Connect and bind evidence handles (monitoring, reporting, audit, orchestration, Digital Soul, workforce, commerce, knowledge, programme certification, optional runtimes).
2. Evaluate system health from injected monitoring / recovery / orchestration evidence only.
3. Evaluate performance, business, AI workforce, and self-awareness intelligence — honest no-evidence when unbound.
4. Detect deterioration against prior persistent awareness state; never silent on critical deltas.
5. Investigate root causes; estimate business impact and urgency from evidence.
6. Generate prioritized executive recommendations (`autoApplyForbidden: true`).
7. Escalate unacknowledged findings until acknowledged; preserve escalation history.
8. Produce Situational Awareness Reports and Grand King briefings.
9. Submit reports through Executive Reporting Runtime when bound.
10. Preserve immutable awareness and audit history; expose cockpit snapshot and diagnostics.

- NEVER bypass Pillow / Grand King
