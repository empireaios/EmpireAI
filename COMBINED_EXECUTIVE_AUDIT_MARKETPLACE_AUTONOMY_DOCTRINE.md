## 1. Executive Summary

REAL-051A **Marketplace Autonomy Doctrine** is adopted as the permanent EmpireAI Version 1 commercial operating model. The repository now formally states:

- **Founder one-time onboarding** on third-party platforms only — no repetitive manual operational work after onboarding.
- **Marketplace autonomy** — EmpireAI executes publish, sync, route, fulfil, and monitor after approved credentials are stored.
- **Marketplace strategy** — common products to marketplaces; premium/branded products to dedicated Shopify stores.
- **Approval chain preserved** — Grand King + executive governance + valid production credentials before automation.
- **Generic future expansion** — adapter-agnostic; no platform-specific governance redesign.

**Certification:** Doctrine adopted · repository synchronized · constitutional compliance maintained · **no governance regressions** · **no runtime behavior changed**.
