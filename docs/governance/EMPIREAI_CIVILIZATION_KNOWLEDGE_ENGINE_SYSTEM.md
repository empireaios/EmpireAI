# Civilization Knowledge Engine

Doctrine: PILLOW-CKE-001. Mission: X5-16.

The Civilization Knowledge Engine establishes continuous external strategic knowledge integration. It monitors industries, technologies, scientific, economic, and regulatory developments, and business innovations; identifies emerging strategic knowledge; ranks strategic relevance; and generates strategic knowledge recommendations.

It produces machine-readable civilization knowledge records with status, health, and failure reporting. It never exposes credentials or authentication tokens, and never integrates unvalidated external knowledge into enterprise decision-making automatically. Knowledge traceability, auditability, and enterprise integrity are preserved.
