## Constitutional Purpose

Knowledge is one of the Empire's greatest assets. Knowledge shall never be lost and shall continuously mature. Every mission, recovery, business outcome, and architectural decision permanently strengthens the Empire through validated, evidence-based knowledge evolution.

## Knowledge Evolution Pipeline

Mission Completed → Evidence Collected → Knowledge Extraction → Classification → Validation → Knowledge Integration → Repository Update → Vision Accumulation → Future Mission Improvement

## Knowledge Principles

Knowledge First · Evidence First · Constitution First · No Duplicate Truth · No Knowledge Loss · Continuous Learning · Continuous Improvement · Traceable Knowledge
