## Purpose

The Revenue Acceleration Engine module provides intelligent revenue acceleration and revenue optimization for EmpireAI. It monitors revenue growth, trends, product revenue, channel revenue, and customer revenue; identifies acceleration opportunities and bottlenecks; optimizes revenue strategies; ranks opportunities; and recommends revenue acceleration using structural signals only — never recommending revenue actions without validated supporting data.
