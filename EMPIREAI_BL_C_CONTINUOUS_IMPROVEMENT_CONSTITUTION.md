# EmpireAI — Business Law C (BL-C)

## Continuous Improvement Constitution

**Version:** 1  
**Canonical label:** BL-C · Continuous Improvement Constitution  
**Status:** **ACTIVE** — accumulates approved continuous-improvement items  
**Authority:** EmpireAI Constitution (supreme) · BL-C derives authority from Constitution  
**Date:** 2026-06-29  
**Canonical owner:** Repository Governance · Continuous Improvement

> BL-C governs **continuous improvement** — not engineering execution.  
> BL-A governs engineering implementation. BL-B governs repository architecture and engineering governance. BL-C complements both.

---

## Authority

The EmpireAI Constitution is the supreme authority.

BL-C derives its authority from the Constitution.

BL-C governs every continuous improvement process throughout EmpireAI.

BL-C governs how EmpireAI evolves after implementation.

BL-C does not govern engineering execution.

BL-C governs improvement.

---

## Purpose

EmpireAI shall never become static.

Every completed implementation becomes the foundation for future improvement.

Continuous improvement shall become a permanent capability of the Empire.

BL-C establishes the doctrine governing that capability.

---

## Core Philosophy

Implementation creates capability.

Improvement creates superiority.

EmpireAI shall continuously become:

- more intelligent
- more automated
- more profitable
- more scalable
- more resilient
- more maintainable
- more autonomous

---

## Continuous Improvement Doctrine

Every completed engineering mission shall produce future improvement opportunities.

Improvement opportunities shall never block mission completion.

Improvement opportunities shall be preserved for future execution.

Every improvement shall originate from repository evidence.

EmpireAI shall continuously improve without continuously redesigning.

---

## Repository First

Every improvement proposal belongs to the repository.

Conversation is temporary.

Repository knowledge is permanent.

Every approved improvement shall eventually exist as a repository artifact.

---

## Enhancement Philosophy

Enhancements represent future value.

Enhancements are not defects.

Enhancements are not mandatory corrections.

Enhancements exist because EmpireAI continuously evolves.

EmpireAI shall distinguish between:

- Required Corrections
- Architecture Corrections
- Repository Corrections
- Future Enhancements
- Commercial Opportunities
- Automation Opportunities
- Engineering Opportunities

---

## Enhancement Principles

Every enhancement shall satisfy all of the following:

| Principle | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Evidence Based | Traceable origin |
| Repository Traceable | Canonical artifact location |
| Owner Assigned | Explicit repository owner |
| Priority Classified | Critical / High / Normal / Low / Future |
| Repository Governed | BL-C lifecycle |
| Executive Audited | Future Enhancements section |
| Synchronizable | Journey + register sync |
| Future Implementable | Separate mission approval |

---

## Enhancement Categories

Enhancements may belong to one or more categories.

Examples include: UX · Pillow · Repository · Architecture · Commercial · Automation · Governance · Security · Performance · Scalability · Reliability · Developer Experience · Recovery · Journey · Executive Audit · Global Components · Executive Components · REAL Owners · Artificial Intelligence.

Future categories shall automatically become supported.

---

## Canonical Enhancement Registers

EmpireAI shall maintain canonical enhancement registers.

**Initially active (BL-C v1):**

| Register | Canonical path | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| UX Enhancement Register | `docs/governance/UX_ENHANCEMENT_REGISTER.md` | UX Governance |
| Pillow Enhancement Register | `docs/governance/PILLOW_ENHANCEMENT_REGISTER.md` | Pillow Architecture |

**Future registers may include:** REAL · Commercial · Automation · Repository · Architecture · Executive Component · Global Component · Security · Performance · Scalability enhancement registers.

Enhancement registers shall never become duplicated. Journey shall reference enhancement registers. Executive Audits shall reference enhancement registers.

---

## Enhancement Lifecycle

Every enhancement shall follow the same lifecycle:

```
Observation → Evidence → Proposal → Repository Registration → Priority Assessment
      → Grand King Approval → Mission Planning → Engineering → Executive Audit
      → Repository Synchronization → Completed Enhancement
```

No enhancement shall bypass this lifecycle.

Enhancement registration shall **never** modify engineering implementation. Implementation requires separate approval.

---

## Executive Audit Doctrine

Every completed engineering mission shall evaluate future enhancements.

Executive Audits shall distinguish:

- Acceptance Criteria
- Mandatory Corrections
- Repository Corrections
- Future Enhancements

Enhancements shall never be mixed with acceptance criteria.

Every Executive Audit **shall** contain a section titled **Future Enhancements** (per `EMPIREAI_EXECUTIVE_AUDIT_STANDARD.md` §2).

---

## Mission Rule

Every completed UX mission shall update the UX Enhancement Register.

Every completed Pillow mission shall update the Pillow Enhancement Register.

Future engineering programs shall update their respective enhancement registers.

This doctrine becomes mandatory across EmpireAI.

---

## Grand King Authority

The Grand King remains the sole authority for approving enhancement implementation.

Enhancement registration does not authorize engineering execution.

Approval remains mandatory.

---

## Enhancement Priority

Priority levels: **Critical** · **High** · **Normal** · **Low** · **Future**

Priority shall consider: commercial impact · engineering impact · repository impact · architecture impact · risk reduction · automation value · technical debt reduction · future scalability · long-term maintainability.

---

## Enhancement Ownership

Every enhancement shall identify a repository owner. Unowned enhancements are prohibited.

Possible owners include: UX · Pillow · REAL Owner · Global Component · Executive Component · Commercial · Repository · Architecture · Journey · Executive Audit · Automation · Recovery · future repository owners.

---

## Evidence Requirements

Every enhancement proposal shall include evidence from: Executive Audit · Repository Review · Architecture Review · Continuous Due Diligence · Commercial Analysis · Engineering Review · Repository Health · Dependency Analysis · Journey Review · Grand King instruction · repository synchronization.

No enhancement shall exist without traceable evidence.

---

## Duplicate Prevention

When similar enhancements exist, they shall be **Merged** · **Referenced** · **Superseded** · **Retired**. Repository duplication shall be avoided.

---

## Enhancement Status

Statuses: Observed · Proposed · Reviewed · Approved · Deferred · Scheduled · In Progress · Completed · Rejected · Retired · Superseded.

History shall never be deleted.

---

## Enhancement Retirement

Enhancements may be retired when: implementation completed · architecture replaced · requirement removed · superseded · repository restructuring. Retirement shall preserve repository history.

---

## Enhancement Promotion

Approved enhancements may become engineering missions. Promotion requires: repository evidence · owner validation · dependency validation · mission planning · Grand King approval. Promotion shall never occur automatically.

---

## Relationship with Mission Planner

Mission Planner shall consider enhancement registers when generating future missions. Enhancements become candidates. Mission Planner determines execution order. Mission Planner shall never ignore approved enhancement registers.

---

## Relationship with Continuous Due Diligence

Continuous Due Diligence shall continuously discover new enhancements. New findings shall enter the appropriate enhancement register. No enhancement shall bypass repository registration.

---

## Relationship with Repository Synchronization

Repository Synchronization shall determine whether enhancement registers require updates. Synchronization shall preserve ordering · ownership · history · repository references · Journey references. Synchronization shall never discard enhancement history.

---

## Autonomous Improvement (Pillow)

Pillow shall continuously discover improvement opportunities. Pillow may recommend repository · architecture · commercial · engineering · automation · governance improvements. Recommendations shall never directly modify the repository. Grand King approval remains mandatory.

---

## Continuous Learning

EmpireAI shall learn from: completed missions · Executive Audits · repository history · Journey history · commercial outcomes · architecture evolution · enhancement implementation · repository synchronization. Learning shall improve future recommendations. Learning shall never rewrite repository history.

---

## Executive Review

Major enhancement groups shall be reviewed during: UX Master Executive Audit · Pillow Master Executive Audit · EmpireAI Master Executive Audit.

---

## BL-C Governance

BL-C governs continuous improvement only.

BL-C does not authorize engineering implementation.

BL-C does not replace BL-A or BL-B.

BL-C complements the EmpireAI Constitution.

| Release | Role |
|---|---|
| BL-A | Engineering implementation synchronization |
| BL-B | Repository architecture and engineering governance |
| BL-C | Continuous improvement and enhancement ecosystem |

---

## Grand King Doctrine

The Grand King determines which improvements become reality.

EmpireAI proposes. Pillow analyzes. Executive Audits evaluate. Repository preserves. The Grand King decides.
