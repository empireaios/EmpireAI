# EMPIREAI UX & IDENTITY DOCTRINE — UID-001 → UID-020

> **Immutable.** Version 1.0.0  
> This document is the permanent User Experience and Identity Doctrine of EmpireAI.  
> It is **NOT** runtime logic. It governs how EmpireAI is experienced.  
> Every screen must improve decision-making, not decoration. Empire Review validates compliance.

**Canonical source:** `backend/src/foundation/empire-ux-identity-doctrine/catalog/uid-catalog.ts`  
**API:** `GET /empire-ux-identity-doctrine/catalog` · `GET /empire-ux-identity-doctrine/navigation-review` · `GET /empire-ux-identity-doctrine/compliance`

---

## UID-001 — Grand King Platform Owner

Grand King is the Platform Owner. Grand King is not a selectable role. Grand King logs in through Founder authentication using Grand King credentials and is automatically recognized by EmpireAI.

## UID-002 — Founder Is Tenant

Founder is a tenant. Founder never sees Grand King capabilities.

## UID-003 — No Role Selection

No role-selection screen. Authentication determines destination.

## UID-004 — Separate Dashboards

Grand King dashboard must always be completely separate from Founder dashboard.

## UID-005 — What Should I Do Next

Every dashboard must answer: What should I do next?

## UID-006 — Business Decisions

Every page must contribute to business decisions.

## UID-007 — No Display-Only Dashboards

No dashboard exists only to display data.

## UID-008 — Mission Home Headquarters

Mission Home is Empire Headquarters. Everything important begins here.

## UID-009 — Executive Headquarters

Executive Headquarters must present the Empire, not individual modules.

## UID-010 — Standard Navigation Model

Country → Marketplace → Products → Performance → Executive Recommendation is the standard navigation model.

## UID-011 — Instant Global Health

Grand King must immediately understand global business health within seconds of login.

## UID-012 — Visual Executive Debates

Executive debates must be visual. Not logs. Not chat.

## UID-013 — Soul After Debate

Soul recommendations must always appear after Executive debate.

## UID-014 — King Actions Visible

Grand King actions Approve, Reject, Request Investigation must always be visible.

## UID-015 — Business Over Technical

Business health must take priority over technical health.

## UID-016 — Revenue Profit Risk Priority

Visual hierarchy must prioritize Revenue, Profit, Risk, Opportunities before secondary metrics.

## UID-017 — Minimize Clicks

Navigation must minimize the number of clicks to reach critical decisions.

## UID-018 — Explain Why

Every important visual must explain WHY not only WHAT.

## UID-019 — Simple Professional UX

Version 1 UX must remain simple, professional, and operational. Avoid unnecessary animations, decorations, or novelty.

## UID-020 — SUCCESS-001 UX Goal

Every UX decision must increase Grand King's ability to operate EmpireAI towards SUCCESS-001 USD 100,000 net profit.
