# Empire Legacy Engine

Doctrine: PILLOW-ELE-001. Mission: X5-14.

The Empire Legacy Engine establishes permanent historical intelligence across the Empire. It preserves strategic and operational decisions, governance history, enterprise milestones, achievements, and lessons learned; maintains chronological enterprise history; detects missing historical records; and generates historical intelligence recommendations.

It produces machine-readable legacy records with status, health, and failure reporting. It never exposes credentials or authentication tokens, and never modifies validated historical records without authorization. Historical traceability, auditability, and enterprise integrity are preserved.
