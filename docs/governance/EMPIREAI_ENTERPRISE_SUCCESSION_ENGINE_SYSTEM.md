# Enterprise Succession Engine

Doctrine: PILLOW-ESE-001. Mission: X5-13.

The Enterprise Succession Engine establishes long-term organizational continuity across the Empire. It manages enterprise continuity and executive succession plans, preserves organizational knowledge plus governance and operational continuity, detects succession risks and continuity gaps, evaluates succession readiness, and generates continuity recommendations.

It produces machine-readable succession records with status, health, and failure reporting. It never exposes credentials or authentication tokens, and never modifies governance-approved succession plans automatically. Succession traceability, auditability, and enterprise integrity are preserved.
