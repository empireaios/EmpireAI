# EmpireAI Organization Charter System

PILLOW-OCH-001 / Q1-02 provides the Organization Charter.

The Organization Charter formally defines how the AI Workforce is organized under Pillow. It establishes the official organizational hierarchy, reporting relationships, authority boundaries, departmental responsibilities, and escalation paths.

Every AI Worker, Department, Factory and Executive component must belong to this organizational structure. Pillow is the sole Executive Authority over the entire organization.

> Note: Doctrine ID is **PILLOW-OCH-001**. There is one authoritative Organization Charter. Every future AI Worker, Department and Factory must register under this charter.

## Boundaries

The Organization Charter:

- **does** define organizational structure, reporting hierarchy, authority, responsibility, and escalation
- does **not** execute worker tasks
- does **not** replace Workforce Operating System
- does **not** replace Workforce Orchestrator
- does **not** override Pillow
- does **not** override Grand King

## Organization Charter definition

Each definition includes: Charter Version, Executive Authority, Organizational Hierarchy, Departments, Factories, Reporting Relationships, Authority Levels, Responsibility Matrix, Escalation Hierarchy, Governance Rules, and Metadata version (`OCH-001-v1`).

## Mandatory organizational rules

Default: Pillow is the supreme executive authority; every worker belongs to exactly one department; every department belongs to one factory; every factory reports to Pillow; every worker has a reporting chain; every responsibility has an owner; every escalation reaches Pillow if unresolved; no worker operates outside the organization.

Additional departments and factories can be registered through configuration without redesigning the charter.

## Safety

Credentials and authentication tokens are never exposed. Charter operations preserve auditability and traceability. Sensitive values are masked in logs. Organization records never claim that the charter executed worker tasks, replaced Workforce Operating System, replaced Workforce Orchestrator, overrode Pillow, or overrode Grand King.
