## Purpose



The Profit Scaling Engine module provides profit-first enterprise scaling for EmpireAI. It monitors profit growth, gross margin, net margin, operating margin, scaling costs, and ROI; detects profit erosion and unprofitable growth; optimizes profit during scaling; and recommends profit-preserving scale using structural signals only — never prioritizing growth over validated profitability.
